    The 4 golden rules

    • 1. Zero heel or leg driveDo not push into the floor or foot pegs to lift more. Let the machine backrest support the load.
    • 2. Keep the left leg neutralKeep it straight and un-angled. Do not tuck the foot under the seat or rotate the ankle or knee outward.
    • 3. No standing weight shiftFor cable work, use soft knees and distribute weight evenly. Never lean or shift onto the left leg.
    • 4. No leg movement under core tensionSkip bicycles, mountain climbers, and leg raises while recovering.

    This guide is informational, not medical advice. Stop if you feel pain, warmth, pulling, or instability around the outer hip or knee, and check with your physical therapist or clinician.
