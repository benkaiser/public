# Kids Photo Gallery — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 October 2026

Kids Photo Gallery is a gallery for young children, set up and supervised by a
parent or guardian. This policy describes the current app's handling of photos,
videos and settings.

## Your photos and videos

Kids Photo Gallery displays photos and videos from the device photo library
with the permission you grant. A parent chooses which albums to show and
whether photos or videos are enabled. On supported systems you can limit the
library items the app can access. The app may read library metadata such as
album identifiers and media dates to browse and display the permitted items.

The app reads permitted photos, videos and metadata on your device to display
them; it does not send them to the app's developer or a developer-operated
service. The current app has no accounts, advertising, tracking, analytics or
in-app purchases. If an iOS item exists only in your iCloud library, Apple's
photo-library services may download it when you view it. This uses your Apple
account and device settings and may use mobile data; Apple's terms and privacy
policy apply to that service.

## On-device storage

Photo/video preferences and selected album identifiers are stored locally
using the operating system's preference storage. Media libraries and playback
components may create on-device thumbnails, converted files or cached copies
to display or play permitted content. Platform backup behavior is governed by
your device settings; “local” does not promise exclusion from OS-managed backups.

## Your choices

The app checks existing permission at startup without displaying a permission
request. A parent starts the photo-access request through the parent challenge.
Change displayed albums and media toggles behind the same challenge. The app
starts with no albums selected until a parent chooses what to show.

Parents can open system photo-permission Settings through a gated action in
the app, or visit Settings directly. On supported systems, parents can choose
limited access rather than the entire library. Revoke or limit access there
at any time. Clearing app data or
uninstalling removes app-local settings/caches according to the platform;
it does not delete the photos and videos in your system library. Any device or
iCloud backups are managed separately by you and the platform provider.

## Children and parents

The gallery is intended for young children with parent or guardian setup.
Parents are responsible for selecting suitable media. The reading-based challenge
is not strong authentication, and full-screen presentation is not a device lock.
Use Guided Access or app pinning when device containment is needed.

## Public pages and support

The [support page](https://cdn.benkaiser.dev/kids-photo-gallery/support.html)
explains setup and recovery. Support and privacy questions can be directed to
the Kids Photo Gallery maintainers through
[public support issue form](https://github.com/benkaiser/public/issues/new?title=Kids%20Photo%20Gallery%20support).
Issues are public: do not include a child's photos or videos, personal
information, passwords, or private library details.

Opening these pages or GitHub takes you to an external web service. Your browser
and those services handle web requests under their own privacy policies.
Information you choose to submit to GitHub is separate from the app's on-device
photo viewing; the app does not submit support reports automatically.

## Contact and changes

Use the GitHub issues link above for non-sensitive privacy questions. If the
app's data handling changes, this policy will be updated with a new effective
date. Review the policy associated with the version you use.
