# Endless Maze Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 28 September 2026

Endless Maze is a maze game for children and families. It is designed to be played completely offline.

## Short version

Endless Maze does not collect, use, sell, share, or transmit personal information from anyone, including children.

## Children’s privacy

Endless Maze is intended to be suitable for children. The app does not ask children for their name, age, location, email address, photos, voice, contacts, or any other personal information. There are no accounts, social features, chat, advertising identifiers, analytics, or behaviour tracking.

Because the app does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## What data is stored on the device?

The game stores one local save file on the device: `user://save.json`.

That save file contains only gameplay progress:

- the world seed used to regenerate the mazes;
- the selected difficulty;
- the chosen explorer;
- the current level; and
- the drawn paths, saved as maze cell numbers for completed mazes and the current in-progress path.

This save file stays on the device. It is not sent to Ben Kaiser, kaiserapps, Apple, Google, or any third party by Endless Maze.

If you delete the app or clear its app data, the local save is removed by the operating system.

## Network, ads, purchases, and third parties

Endless Maze has:

- no network calls;
- no analytics;
- no advertising;
- no in-app purchases;
- no accounts;
- no external links; and
- no third-party SDKs.

## Permissions

The Android build requests vibration so the game can give a small haptic bump when a player pushes into a wall or slips away from the path. This is not used to collect data.
