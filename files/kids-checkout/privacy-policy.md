# Kids Checkout Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 October 2026

Kids Checkout is a pretend-shopping game for children, provided by Kaiser Apps.
It has no accounts, advertising, in-app purchases, analytics or tracking.

## Information handled by the app

The app does not collect or transmit personal information. It does not ask for a
child's name, age, email address, location or other identifying information.

With your permission, the camera is used to recognize grocery barcodes. Camera
frames and barcode recognition are processed on the device. The app does not
save photos or videos, record audio, or upload camera images or scanned barcodes.
ZXing-C++, the barcode decoder, runs locally and does not contact a server.

Scanned product identifiers, pretend cart items, quantities and pretend prices
are held locally during play. There is no purchase, payment processing or
shopping-history service. Cart contents are not saved as a shopping history and
are discarded when the app process ends. The bundled product catalog and its
local working copy on Android contain product data, not information about the
person using the app.

The app reads device language and region settings locally to choose its
interface language and pretend currency. It does not collect location data.
Timing and bundled-file metadata are used locally for scan cooldowns and
resource validation, not to identify or track a device.

## Children's privacy

The app is designed for children and does not knowingly collect personal
information from children. It has no social features, user-generated content,
advertising identifiers or third-party tracking SDKs.

Privacy and support pages are linked from the app's store listing. The in-app
information panel contains offline licenses only, with no external links.
Opening camera permission settings requires a separate grown-up check. That
check is a local calculation; answers are neither saved nor transmitted.

## Permissions and choices

Camera access is optional. Without it, children can still play by tapping food
illustrations. You can grant or revoke camera permission in your device's app
settings. The app does not request microphone, contacts, location, photo-library
or storage permissions.

There is no app-collected personal information to access, correct or delete.
Closing the app process clears its in-memory play session. Uninstalling removes
the app's local files.

## External websites, stores and support

The app itself works offline and does not link to websites or compose support
emails. Privacy/support pages, product-data attribution and catalog downloads
are available outside the app through its store listing or public pages.
A website host, browser, operating system, app store or
email provider may handle connection details such as an IP address under its
own policies. No cart, camera image or scanned barcode is attached by the app.

If you email support, we receive the email address and information you choose
to send. We use it to respond to the request, not for advertising or profiling.
Please have a grown-up contact us and do not send children's personal
information, photos or videos. Support correspondence is kept only as long as
needed to handle the request and any applicable legal obligations. You can ask
us to delete your correspondence.
