# Kids Checkout Support

Kids Checkout is an offline pretend-shopping game for children. All prices and
payments are pretend: the app does not buy groceries or charge money.

## Contact

Please have a grown-up email kidscheckout_support@kaiserapps.com.

Include your device model, operating-system version, app version and a
description of the problem. Do not include children's names, photos, videos or
other personal information. The app does not automatically attach diagnostics,
camera images, scanned barcodes or cart contents to an email.

## Camera and scanning

Camera permission is optional; the food buttons work without it. To enable
scanning, allow camera access in your device's app settings. App links to
settings require a grown-up check.

Use good light, keep the full retail barcode in view and try the rear camera
first. Some front cameras have fixed focus, so moving a product farther away
may help. The app recognizes grocery EAN/UPC and ITF-14 barcodes, not QR codes.

Wait at least one second before scanning the same product again. A repeat adds
quantity to the existing cart item. An unknown barcode receives a stable,
whimsical pretend product rather than making an online lookup.

## Sounds

Sound effects use the device's media/game volume. On iOS they can play even when
the silent switch is enabled. Check media volume and any connected headphones
or Bluetooth audio devices if a sound cannot be heard.

## Privacy and licenses

Hold the small info button in the food-grid header for two seconds to read the
offline third-party licenses. A short tap explains that the button must be held.
The license panel has no external links or parental challenge. Privacy and
support are available through the store listing.
