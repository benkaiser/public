Kids Checkout product catalog

Open Food Facts and its contributors:
https://world.openfoodfacts.org/

This is a modified database, not an official Open Food Facts application.
Modifications include country-balanced selection, deduplication, product-name
cleanup, conservative child-safety filtering, illustration assignments and
generated approximate USD play prices. Prices are pretend, not retail quotes.
No Open Food Facts photographs are included.

The derived products.sqlite database is made available under ODbL 1.0.
